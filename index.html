About Holokleria

Holokleria LLC is a Tennessee Limited Liability Company providing education across three distinct disciplines through dedicated brand divisions. Our mission is grounded in the conviction that genuine education — clinical, personal, and spiritual — moves people toward wholeness and maturity in their field and in their lives.

All educational products, continuing education courses, and content platforms operated by Holokleria LLC are developed with evidence-based rigor, professional integrity, and a commitment to the whole person.

Leadership

Jonathan Crowell, FNP-C

Family Nurse Practitioner — Certified | Master of Divinity

Jonathan Crowell is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner with experience in collegiate nursing education, Emergency Department nursing, Intensive Care nursing, Psychiatric and Mental Health nursing, Community Health nursing, medical missions, and faith-integrated care. He is the founder and sole member of Holokleria LLC and serves as Nurse Planner for FullScope Nursing CE. He is a member of the International Board of Trustees for the Volta Bible College. He is also the West Africa Coordinator for the World Bible Institute serving as an instructor, medical missions director, and partner to the DzoDze Ghana Mission Hospital.
